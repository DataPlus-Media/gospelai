name: Daily study scope
description: Product boundaries for the connected daily Bible study experience.
---
Preserve GospelAI branding, AI chat, free guest entry and existing authentication. Study progress is local-only; do not claim cloud sync.

**Why:** The user specified an incremental first release, not a new app or an authentication/backend replacement.

**How to apply:** New reading features must remain usable without sign-in. Do not introduce cloud-sync claims without separately implementing and agreeing on that scope.

Bible Standard is inspiration only. Do not directly integrate it, copy its assets, or collect its library. Additional translations and commentary need separate sourcing, licensing and product decisions.

**Why:** The user explicitly excluded these from this release; the public website was reviewed, but its native app was not tested.

**How to apply:** Keep bundled KJV reading separate from AI explanation and obtain separate approval and sources for expanded libraries.
